Common molecular-biology factors

dsDNA ≈ 660 g/mol per bp; ssDNA ≈ 330 g/mol per nt; RNA ≈ 340 g/mol per nt.

Approximate A260 conversion factors: dsDNA 50 µg/mL, RNA 40 µg/mL, ssDNA 33 µg/mL at A260=1 for a 1 cm path.

Unit discipline

Keep units consistent before calculation. Convert µL↔mL, nM↔M, ng↔g, rpm↔rad/s and °C↔K where equations require SI units.
